# Source and differences

Adapted from [Slate's presentation-deck skill](https://github.com/prabinpebam/slate/blob/d594742a1a6582c5cb890fcb18e63bc798ced99e/presentation/SKILL.md)
and its slide composition principles, at commit
`d594742a1a6582c5cb890fcb18e63bc798ced99e`.

The narrative spine, concise claims, visual arguments and talking tracks remain.
This independently maintained version replaces the Slate component/runtime contract
with existing-tool or semantic-HTML authoring and includes its own outline template.
It neither bundles nor requires Slate, motion or another skill.
