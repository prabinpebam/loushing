# Glossary

One term per concept. Use the term in the first column everywhere a person reads it: the interface, help, errors, docs. Lower case unless it's a proper name.

| Term | Means | Use instead of |
|---|---|---|
| <page> | <one thing a reader opens, with its own address> | post, entry, article (unless it's a kind of page) |
| <save> | <write the change on this device> | store, persist, commit |
| <publish> | <make it visible to everyone> | deploy, push, go live |

## Banned in the interface

OK · Submit · click here · please · simply · just · oops · invalid (say what's wrong instead) · error <code> on its own
