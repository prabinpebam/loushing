A model sheet of the supporting cast for Chopper, the small fluffy dog in the reference character sheet. Do not draw Chopper himself. Draw in exactly the sheet's art style: a loose, sketchy, hand-drawn ink line, slightly uneven, the same line weight as the sheet, flat soft fill, very light shading, muted colours. Isolated on a transparent background, no ground shadows, no backdrop, no frames, no text, no letters, no labels. Arrange five characters in two rows with wide empty transparent space between every drawing, each drawn twice in two different poses side by side, all at true scale to Chopper as he appears on the sheet. Top row: (1) a small grey-brown squirrel with a big bushy tail, about two-thirds of Chopper's sitting height, quick and cheeky: once sitting up holding an acorn, once mid-scamper; (2) two plump grey pigeons with a green-violet sheen on the neck, calm and entirely unbothered: once standing side by side, once one pigeon alone looking over its shoulder; (3) a small brown sparrow about the size of Chopper's head: once perched, once taking off with wings spread. Bottom row: (4) a round, low, grey robot vacuum with one small light on top, a little wider than Chopper is long: once from a three-quarter view, once from the side; (5) a small pale-yellow butterfly with soft grey markings, about the size of Chopper's ear: once wings open, once wings half closed.
