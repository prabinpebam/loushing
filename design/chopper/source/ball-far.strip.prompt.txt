Chopper, the small fluffy dog in the reference character sheet. Keep him exactly on model: a cream and ivory fluffy coat with warm beige shading; long, feathery, solid black floppy ears; a black saddle patch on his back (larger and mid-back on his right side, smaller and near the shoulder on his left side), exactly as on the sheet; round black bead eyes; a small black nose; a plumed tail curled over his back; a tufty fringe on his head; the same proportions as the sheet. The same art style as the sheet: a loose, sketchy, hand-drawn ink line, slightly uneven, with flat soft fill and very light shading. Only the figures and the listed props. Isolated on a transparent background, no ground shadow, no backdrop, no frame, no border, no text, no letters, no labels, no logos. Scene, a wide panorama read left to right: on the far left, Chopper is in a play bow, front down and tail up, staring hopefully to the right, playful. On the far right, his small coral ball, a little smaller than his head (his favourite; the same ball in every scene), has rolled to a stop, with three short motion lines behind it. The long empty space between them is the joke. Canvas 2304x768 pixels. Draw Chopper small: at most 340 px from nose to tail, his head about 90 px tall from fringe to chin, and an ink line about 3.5 px wide; every other figure and prop at true scale to him. Leave everything else fully transparent and empty.
